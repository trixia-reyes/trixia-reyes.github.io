01 About

A psychologist who teaches, researches and develops people

I'm a psychology educator with 7+ years in higher education and an M.A. in Psychology (Clinical) from Adamson University. I teach core and advanced psychology courses, supervise undergraduate research, and help my department meet PACUCOA accreditation standards. As a Certified Human Resource Associate, I bring the same evidence-based approach to how organizations hire, train and support their people.

In the classroom

Evidence-based teaching, close research mentorship and licensure preparation. I want students to leave with the skills, confidence and ethics to practise psychology well.

In the workplace

Training design and delivery, well-being programs, quality assurance and stakeholder coordination. I use psychology to help teams learn faster and stay well.
